# Safety — read this first

🇬🇧 English · [🇵🇱 Polski](SAFETY.pl.md)

A helicopter is a machine whose failures are often fatal. A rotor several metres in diameter, a high-voltage battery storing over ten kWh, and flying an uncertified design are three independent sources of lethal risk.

## Project rules

1. **No warranty.** Everything here is provided "as is". The authors accept no liability. You build and fly at your own risk and in accordance with the law of your country.
2. **Every file has a status:** 💭 idea · 📐 calculated · 🧪 measured · ✅ approved. Only "approved" may be used for flight-critical parts — and "approved" means team consensus, not certification.
3. **Staged testing.** No tests with a person on board until the design has passed: bench tests → tethered tests without a pilot → uncrewed flights.
4. **Keep people clear.** During rotor tests nobody stands in the rotor plane or inside the defined safety zone.
5. **High voltage.** Battery and powertrain work only by qualified people, with insulated tools, a service disconnect and a lockout/tagout (LOTO) procedure.
6. **Li-ion batteries.** Charge supervised, on a non-flammable surface, with a fire plan (thermal runaway — ordinary extinguishers may not be enough).
7. **Report problems.** Any issue that may affect safety → open an Issue using the **"Safety issue"** template. It has top priority.
