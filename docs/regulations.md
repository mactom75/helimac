## EU: Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2018/1139

The EASA Basic Regulation does not apply to the aircraft categories listed in its Annex I — these fall under **national rules**. They include aeroplanes and helicopters with no more than two seats whose maximum take-off mass (MTOM) does not exceed:

| Variant | MTOM limit |
|---|---|
| Single-seat (land) | 300 kg |
| Extra for float/amphibian version (single-seat) | +30 kg |
| Extra for airframe-mounted total recovery parachute (single-seat) | +15 kg |
| Two-seat (land) | 450 kg |

Separately, any manned aircraft with a **maximum empty mass ≤ 70 kg** (including fuel) is outside EASA's scope — practically unreachable for an electric helicopter with a pilot.

Sources:
- [Regulation (EU) 2018/1139 — Easy Access Rules, Annex I (EASA)](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/easy-access-rules/online-publications/regulation-eu-20181139-european-parliament?page=10)
- [Summary of Annex I limits (IAA, Ireland)](https://www.iaa.ie/general-aviation/aircraft-categories/helicopters/aircraft-(incl-helicopters)-not-affected-by-easa-regulations)
